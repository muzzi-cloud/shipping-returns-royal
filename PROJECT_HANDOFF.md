# Client Handoff Checklist

## Before sending to a client
- [ ] Replace Royal name/logo if this is for another business.
- [ ] Confirm all product names, models, prices, discounts, stock and compatibility.
- [ ] Replace remote sample photos with images of the exact product, with permission.
- [ ] Add the actual phone, WhatsApp, email, address, business hours and service area.
- [ ] Confirm domain and hosting owner/access.
- [ ] Confirm who maintains product updates and who handles orders, returns and customer support.

## Before accepting real orders
- [ ] Backend and database deployed
- [ ] Secure checkout/payment provider integrated
- [ ] Server-side validation and order confirmation
- [ ] Inventory, shipping charges and delivery zones configured
- [ ] Real privacy, returns, warranty and terms policies reviewed
- [ ] Test orders, cancellation, refund and failure scenarios completed
- [ ] Backups, access controls, monitoring and update process set up

## Suggested client discovery questions
1. Which exact products and device models do you sell?
2. How many products should launch with the site?
3. Do you want WhatsApp enquiries, cash on delivery, online payments, or a mix?
4. Which cities or countries do you deliver to, and what are the charges?
5. How will stock, prices, returns and customer messages be managed?
6. Do you already own a domain, logo, product photos and hosting?
7. Who will maintain the website after launch?

## Scope clarity
A static demo can demonstrate the visual design and basic browser interactions. It is not the same as a fully operational online store. Estimate backend, payment integration, content entry, hosting, legal policy work and ongoing maintenance separately.

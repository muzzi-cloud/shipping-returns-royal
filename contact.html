A FEW USEFUL NOTES

Before you order.

How do I check if an accessory fits my phone?

Confirm the exact device model and connector type. For cases and screen protectors, model names and generations matter; for chargers, check the connector, supported charging standard and wattage.

Are the prices and stock live?

No. This is a demonstration storefront with sample product names and prices. A real store needs verified inventory, current prices and an order-management process.

Can I pay online here?

Not in this demo. Do not enter payment details. A production checkout requires a trusted payment provider, server-side validation and clear refund and privacy policies.

How do delivery and returns work?

These policies must be set by the real business before launch, including delivery areas, estimated times, return eligibility, condition requirements and refund timelines.

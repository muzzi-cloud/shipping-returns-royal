CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Shipping & returns.

A policy template to customise for the actual store before launch.

Important: this is a template.

These are not an established business policy. Replace every placeholder and get appropriate local advice before accepting orders.

Delivery

Define the locations served, delivery partner, dispatch timeframe, delivery fee, estimated transit time and what happens if a delivery attempt fails. State whether cash on delivery is available and how the customer is contacted.

Returns and exchanges

Specify the return window, eligible and excluded products, required packaging, proof of purchase, process for reporting a damaged or incorrect item, and who pays return shipping.

Refunds

Explain how refunds are approved, which payment method is used, expected processing times and how customers can follow up.

Warranty and compatibility

Clearly distinguish manufacturer warranty, seller warranty and products sold without a warranty. Avoid making claims that cannot be verified.

Contact

Add the real business email, phone number, WhatsApp contact and operating hours.
